How it works

Four simple steps

  1. Get in touch

    Call 313-264-4094 or send us a message and tell us what you need.

  2. Share your documents

    We'll tell you exactly what to send and how to send it securely.

  3. Review together

    We prepare your return and go through it with you before anything is filed.

  4. File and follow up

    We file, track your refund and stay available for questions all year.

What to bring

  • Photo ID for you (and your spouse if filing jointly)
  • Social Security cards for everyone on the return
  • W-2s, 1099s and any other income forms
  • Last year's tax return
  • Bank routing and account number for direct deposit
  • Receipts for childcare, education and other deductions
  • Business income and expense records, if self-employed

Not sure what applies to you? Ask us. We'll make you a personal list.
